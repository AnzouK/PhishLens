02What it checks

Real bank and university emails use the same words as phishing, so a text model on its own flags them. PhishLens checks the evidence before it decides.

  • WordingDistilBERT, fine-tuned on about 29,500 emails, scores the text; LIME shows the words that pushed the verdict.
  • LinksEvery real link target, including the one behind "Click here", checked against Safe Browsing, PhishTank, URLhaus and Spamhaus.
  • SenderSPF, DKIM and DMARC show who really sent it. Forwarded mail does not borrow the forwarder's trust.
  • AttachmentsPDFs (scanned ones through OCR), QR codes, Office macros and remote templates, ZIP, RAR and 7z archives and calendar invites.
  • Real inboxesConversations, image-only mail and links to files shared on Drive, OneDrive or Dropbox.

03Features

  • Explainable verdictsA score per check, the signals found, and the words that pushed the text model, so you can judge for yourself.
  • Gmail and OutlookA Scan button next to the subject in Gmail and Outlook on the web; files and pasted text in the popup.
  • Automatic checksOptional: new mail is checked as it arrives, marked Safe or Phishing? in the list, with a notification.
  • Private by designNo account, nothing stored on the server, history kept in your browser. Run your own backend and nothing leaves your machine.
  • Your own accuracyMark each verdict right or wrong and see how PhishLens performs on your real mail, with a CSV export.
  • Open and testedMIT licensed, with a threat model, CodeQL and more than 170 automated tests run on every change.

04How it works

  1. ReadThe text, the real link targets, the sender and, in Gmail, the full original headers.
  2. ScoreDistilBERT for the wording, two Random Forests for the links and the headers.
  3. VerifyAuthentication results and four threat-intelligence sources, before any verdict.
  4. ExplainA verdict, a score per check, the signals found and the words behind it, above the email.

06Install

The Chrome Web Store listing is in preparation. Until then, load the extension from the repository: about two minutes.

1. The extension

  1. Download the latest release (Source code, zip) and unzip it, or clone the repository.
  2. Open chrome://extensions and turn on Developer mode (top right).
  3. Click Load unpacked and select the extension/ folder.
  4. Open an email in Gmail or Outlook on the web and click Scan with PhishLens next to the subject.

2. A backend

  1. PhishLens Cloud (default): nothing to do.
  2. Your own, private: run it with Docker, then pick Local in the extension settings.
huggingface-cli download AnzouKiona/phishlens-distilbert \
  --local-dir backend/model
cd backend && docker compose up --build

Python without Docker, configuration and deployment: see the quickstart.
