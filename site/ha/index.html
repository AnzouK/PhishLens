Abin da yake dubawa

Imel na gaske daga bankuna da jami'o'i suna amfani da kalmomi iri ɗaya da phishing, don haka samfurin rubutu shi kaɗai yana yi musu alama. PhishLens yana duba shaida kafin ya yanke hukunci.

  • RubutuSamfuri mai harsuna da yawa (mmBERT-small, an ciro shi daga babban mai gano phishing) yana ba rubutu maki a harsuna 22; LIME yana nuna kalmomin da suka karkatar da hukuncin.
  • Hanyoyin haɗiKowace ainihin hanyar haɗi, har da wadda ke bayan "Danna nan", ana duba ta a Safe Browsing, PhishTank, URLhaus da Spamhaus.
  • Mai aikawaSPF, DKIM da DMARC suna nuna wanda ya aiko shi da gaske. Imel da aka tura ba ya ɗaukar amincewar wanda ya tura shi.
  • Haɗe-haɗePDF (har da waɗanda aka ɗauki hoto, ta OCR), lambobin QR, macros da samfuran nesa na Office, ma'ajiyar ZIP, RAR da 7z, da gayyatar kalanda.
  • Ainihin akwatunan saƙoTattaunawa, imel na hotuna kawai, da hanyoyin haɗi zuwa fayilolin da aka raba a Drive, OneDrive ko Dropbox.

Dalilin da ya sa yake akwai

PhishLens ya fara ne a matsayin aikin ƙarshen shekara na ƙungiyarmu a Nile University of Nigeria. Daga cikin batutuwan da muka duba, mun zaɓi mai gano phishing da ke bayyana hukuncinsa maimakon kawai yi wa imel alama. Bayan kammala karatun, na ci gaba da shi ni kaɗai: yanzu aiki ne mai zaman kansa, ba shi da alaƙa da jami'a. A tsakiyar Oktoba yana yi wa imel na halal 130 cikin 297 na jerin aikawa na benchmark ɗinsa alama; gyare-gyaren da suka rage hakan zuwa 1 an bayyana su mataki-mataki, kuma ana wallafa kowane gwaji, mai kyau ko mara kyau.

Girkawa

PhishLens bai riga ya shiga Chrome Web Store ba: ka loda shi da hannu, yana ɗaukar kusan minti biyu.

1. Ƙarin

  1. Sauke PhishLens-extension-….zip daga sabuwar siga ka buɗe shi.
  2. Buɗe chrome://extensions ka kunna Developer mode (sama a dama).
  3. Danna Load unpacked ka zaɓi babban fayil ɗin da ka buɗe.
  4. Buɗe imel a Gmail ko Outlook a yanar gizo ka danna Duba da PhishLens kusa da jigo.

2. Sabar

  1. PhishLens Cloud (na asali): babu abin yi.
  2. Taka ta kanka, ta sirri: kunna ta da Docker, sannan ka zaɓi Local a saitunan ƙarin.
# the public English text model; PhishLens Cloud runs the multilingual one
huggingface-cli download AnzouKiona/phishlens-distilbert \
  --local-dir backend/model
cd backend && docker compose up --build

Python ba tare da Docker ba, saituna da girkawa: duba jagorar farawa (da Turanci).
